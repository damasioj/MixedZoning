[h1]Mixed Zoning[/h1]

Adds [b]7 new paintable zone types[/b]. You choose what each one grows: any mix of low- and high-density residential, low- and high-density commercial, industrial and office.

Paint a street with a mixed zone and get houses next to corner shops, apartments above a row of offices, or whatever combination you like.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Open the [b]Zoning[/b] panel. Mixed Zone 1 to 7 appear after the standard zones.
[*][b]Left-click[/b] a mixed zone to paint with it, just like a normal zone.
[*][b]Right-click[/b] a mixed zone to select it and open its settings window. Tick the building types it should grow. Changes apply and save immediately.
[*]Press [b]Esc[/b], or left-click any zone, to close the window.
[*]You can also edit every zone from [b]Options → Mod Settings → Mixed Zoning[/b].
[/list]

[h2]How growth works[/h2]
[list]
[*]Each time a building is about to spawn, the zone picks one of its allowed types, weighted by current demand.
[*]If a zone allows both densities of a type, high density takes over once demand reaches the [b]high-density demand threshold[/b] (default 50, adjustable).
[*]A zone never grows a building type you haven't unlocked yet. Its button stays disabled until at least one of its types is unlocked.
[*]Grown buildings stay valid as long as the zone still allows their type, so they don't get abandoned.
[/list]

[h2]How the zones look[/h2]
Each mixed zone has a fixed look that doesn't change with its settings. On the map, mixed zone squares are drawn turned 45° (as diamonds) and use a checkerboard of two colours, so they're easy to tell apart from standard zones:
[list]
[*]Zone 1: light purple
[*]Zone 2: light green + dark green
[*]Zone 3: light blue + dark blue
[*]Zone 4: light green + light blue
[*]Zone 5: dark green + office blue
[*]Zone 6: office blue + yellow
[*]Zone 7: light purple + yellow
[/list]
The colours only identify the zone. They say nothing about what it grows, so set each zone up however you like.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] (required)
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Saves are safe: mixed zones are stored in the normal zone data, so no extra save data is added.
[*]Mods that replace zone growth entirely (for example 81 Tiles' zone handling or Building Themes) bypass this mod, so mixed zones won't grow in their areas.
[*]If the game changes in a way the mod doesn't recognise, it switches itself off and the game runs unmodified.
[/list]

[h2]Before removing the mod[/h2]
Repaint mixed-zone areas with a standard zone (or dezone them) before you unsubscribe. Without the mod, those squares hold zone values the base game doesn't use.
