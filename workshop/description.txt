[h1]Mixed Zoning[/h1]

Adds [b]7 new customizable zone types[/b] with unique coloring. Ideal for making cities without strict zoning regulations.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Open the [b]Zoning[/b] panel and scroll to the right. Mixed Zone 1 to 7 appear after the standard zones.
[*][b]Left-click[/b] a mixed zone to paint with it.
[*][b]Right-click[/b] a mixed zone to select it and open its settings window and configure the types of buildings allowed for that zone.
[*](Optional) You can set minimum demand for high-density buildings in [b]Options → Mod Settings → Mixed Zoning[/b]. By default low or high density buildings will be chosen at random.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] (required)
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Mods that replace zone growth entirely (for example 81 Tiles' zone handling or Building Themes) bypass this mod, so mixed zones won't grow in their areas.
[/list]

[h2]Before removing the mod[/h2]
Repaint mixed-zone areas with a standard zone (or dezone them) before you unsubscribe. Without the mod, those squares hold zone values the base game doesn't use.
